## Ecommerce built by a Shopify Partner [.text-h4]

We are Shopify Partners and have built ecommerce stores for fashion, wellness,
home and specialty brands selling in Mexico and the United States. That
experience lets us tell you clearly what your store needs and what it does not,
without adding features you will never use.

Before quoting, we review how you sell: how many products you carry, what
variants they have, how you charge and how you deliver orders. With that
information, the proposal states which configurations the store includes, how
many products will be loaded and what we need from your team.

## The store is yours, not ours [.text-h4]

The Shopify account is opened in your company's name and the main credentials
stay with you. Before launch, you review the full catalog and place a test
order to confirm everything works as agreed.

- Shopify account registered to your company.
- Catalog review and a test order before launch.
- Shopify's own fees listed separately from the cost of our work.
- Your team can run the store without depending on us.
