## What we set up in your store [.text-h4]

Before quoting the project, we review how you sell: how many products you carry, which options they have, how customers pay and how orders are delivered. This lets us explain what the store includes and what information we need from your team.

We are Shopify Partners. We use that experience to prepare a store around your current operation, without adding features you do not need or leaving important decisions until the end.

## How we keep the project clear [.text-h4]

The proposal states how many products are included in the initial setup, what we will configure and when you will review the store. If a new need comes up, we quote it before doing the work.

- Your company owns the Shopify account.
- You review the catalog and checkout before launch.
- Shopify's own fees are separate from the cost of our work.
- Your team keeps the store's main credentials.
