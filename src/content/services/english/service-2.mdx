## Online stores that actually sell [.text-h4]

An online store is not a pretty catalog: it is a sales system. We design the full buying experience, from how a customer finds a product to the moment they receive their order confirmation, taking care of every point where sales are usually lost.

We set up payment gateways, shipping calculation, inventory control and invoicing, and connect your operation with the tools you already use. The result is a store you can run every day without depending on a developer.

## How we approach your store [.text-h4]

We start by understanding your operation: how many products you handle, how you charge, how you ship and who manages the store. Only then do we choose the right platform, instead of forcing a solution that does not fit your business.

- Catalog and data migration from your current platform.
- Setup of payments, taxes, shipping and promotions.
- Funnel analytics to know exactly where sales drop off.
- Training so your team can manage products and orders.
