## What we define before work begins [.text-h4]

We first learn why you need the website and who will use it. We then prepare a proposal listing the pages, features and content required, along with the cost and project schedule.

We do not start with an open-ended list of tasks. If a new request comes up during the project, we explain what it changes and what it costs before doing the work.

## Your company keeps control of its accounts [.text-h4]

The domain, hosting and main accounts remain in your name. We can help set them up and manage them, but you keep the credentials and decide who has access.

- A written scope, cost and schedule.
- Reviews before moving to the next stage.
- Approval before any out-of-scope work begins.
- Main accounts registered to your company.
