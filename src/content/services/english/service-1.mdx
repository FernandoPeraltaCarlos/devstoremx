## Experience behind every website [.text-h4]

We have spent more than seven years building websites for businesses:
informational sites, catalogs and corporate platforms. Part of that experience
comes from coordinating publishing and quality control across more than 30
sites in the international financial sector, where nothing goes live without
being reviewed first. Your website gets that same level of care.

Before quoting, we learn why you need the website and who will use it. We then
prepare a proposal listing the pages, features and content, along with the cost
and schedule. If something new comes up during the project, we explain what it
changes and what it costs before doing the work.

## The accounts stay in your name [.text-h4]

The domain, hosting and main accounts are registered to your company. We can
help set them up and manage them, but you keep the credentials and decide who
has access. If you ever choose to work with another provider, your website is
still yours.

- A written proposal with scope, cost and schedule.
- Reviews before moving to the next stage.
- Approval before any out-of-scope work begins.
- Domain, hosting and accounts registered to your company.
