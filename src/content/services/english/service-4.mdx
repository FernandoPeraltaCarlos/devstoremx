## We start by reviewing what you already have [.text-h4]

A redesign does not always mean starting over. We review the content, how people find important information and how easily your team can update it. We then explain what is worth keeping and what needs to change.

We do not modify the website until you know the scope, cost and order of the work. If a focused update can solve the problem, we tell you instead of recommending a larger project.

## What we agree before changing the website [.text-h4]

The proposal separates what will stay, what will be corrected and what will be rebuilt. It also defines the review stages, so your current website is not changed without your approval.

- A list of changes and affected pages.
- Content your team needs to provide.
- The redesign cost and schedule.
- Reviews before the new version is published.
