## We review first, then propose [.text-h4]

We have worked with many websites that were already live: some needed to be
rebuilt entirely, others only needed targeted fixes. That is why a redesign
starts with a review of what you already have: the content, how easy it is to
find information and how simple it is for your team to keep it up to date.

Based on that review, we tell you what is worth keeping, what to fix and what
to rebuild. If a fix solves the problem, we say so instead of recommending a
bigger project. We do not modify the site until you approve the scope, cost and
order of the work.

## Your current site does not change without your approval [.text-h4]

The proposal separates what stays, what gets fixed and what gets rebuilt, with
defined review stages. The site that is live today keeps running while we
prepare the new version, which is published only once you approve it.

- A review of your current site before quoting.
- A change list separated into keep, fix and rebuild.
- The redesign's cost and schedule in writing.
- The new version published only with your approval.
