## Your site always online and up to date [.text-h4]

A website without maintenance becomes slow, insecure and outdated within months. We make sure that does not happen: we apply updates, review security, run verified backups and monitor availability every day.

We also handle the content changes you need — new sections, copy, images, promotions — with defined response times, so you do not have to learn the admin panel or hire someone full time.

## What you get every month [.text-h4]

This is not just about being available. Every month we document what we did and how your site performed, so you know exactly where the investment is going.

- Uptime, speed and error report.
- Log of updates and backups performed.
- Monitoring of broken links, forms and SSL certificates.
- Improvement recommendations prioritized by impact.
