## We know what it takes to keep sites running [.text-h4]

Our team has coordinated maintenance and quality control for more than 30
corporate sites in the international financial sector, where every change is
reviewed and logged before it goes live. We apply that same discipline to your
website: nothing is modified without first confirming what will be done.

We handle content changes, check forms and verify that recent backups and the
right credentials exist to restore the site if something fails. If a request
requires work beyond what is included, you receive the cost and time estimate
before deciding whether to proceed.

## Every change is logged [.text-h4]

We keep a record of every request we handle, so you know what was changed, when
and why. You can review your site's history at any time, and its operation
never depends on one person's memory.

- Each request confirmed before we work on it.
- Cost quoted upfront when something is not included in the service.
- A log of all changes made.
- Verified backups and credentials under your company's control.
