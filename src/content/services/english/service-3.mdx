## What we can maintain [.text-h4]

We handle content changes, check forms and make the updates needed to keep the information on your website current. We also verify backups and the access needed to restore the website if a problem occurs.

Before doing the work, we confirm whether a request is included in the service. If it requires additional work, you receive the cost and estimated time before deciding whether to proceed.

## Every request has a defined scope [.text-h4]

We do not make changes without first explaining the work involved. We keep a record of completed requests so you know what changed and what remains pending.

- Confirmation before work begins.
- A quoted cost when work is not included.
- A record of completed changes.
- Main accounts under your company's control.
