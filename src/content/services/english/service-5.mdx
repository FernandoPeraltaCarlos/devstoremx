## The types of email we build [.text-h4]

We create templates for campaigns, announcements and messages sent as part of a purchase. The design is prepared for phones and computers and uses your brand's colors, type and images.

Before work begins, we agree on how many templates you need, what content your team will provide and which parts should be editable. This keeps the project from turning into an open-ended list of pieces.

## How the project is delivered [.text-h4]

You review every template before final delivery. The proposal states which edits are included and which files and access details you will receive.

- The agreed number and type of templates.
- Editable content identified upfront.
- Reviews before final delivery.
- Files and access details listed in the proposal.
