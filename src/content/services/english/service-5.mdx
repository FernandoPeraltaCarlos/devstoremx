## Emails that look right on every screen [.text-h4]

We have produced email templates for brands that send campaigns every week:
promotions, newsletters and the messages a customer receives after buying or
when an order ships. An email that looks broken on a phone gets deleted without
being read, so every template is tested on phone and desktop before delivery.

Before starting, we agree on how many templates you need, what content your
team provides and which parts you will be able to edit yourself for each send.
Delivery does not depend on an open-ended list of pieces.

## Delivery defined in the proposal [.text-h4]

We show you each template before preparing the final delivery, and the proposal
specifies the included revisions and the files you will receive. The templates
are loaded into your email platform, under your account, ready to use.

- Number and type of templates agreed in writing.
- Tested on phone and desktop before delivery.
- Editable parts identified from the start.
- Templates installed in your account, not ours.
