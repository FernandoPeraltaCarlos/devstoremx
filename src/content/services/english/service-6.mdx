## One page for a specific campaign [.text-h4]

A landing page puts the information for a product, promotion, event registration or quote request on a single page. It does not replace a complete website: it is prepared for one campaign and one action.

Before designing it, we define what the page needs to explain, what information the form will request and what happens after someone submits it. These points are included in the proposal.

## What is agreed upfront [.text-h4]

The proposal lists the pages, form, connections and reviews included in the project. If the campaign changes and needs new content or features, we explain the adjustment before doing the work.

- The campaign purpose and content.
- Form fields and where submissions are sent.
- Reviews included in the project.
- The account and access used to publish the page.
