## Pages built for one specific campaign [.text-h4]

We have built landing pages for campaigns that receive thousands of visits:
launches, promotions, event registrations and quote requests. A landing page
does not replace a full website; it gathers on a single page everything a
person needs to understand the offer and act on it.

Before designing it, we define what the page must explain, what the form will
ask for and what happens after it is submitted: who receives the information
and what response the visitor sees. All of that is included in the proposal.

## Ready in time for your campaign [.text-h4]

A landing page usually has a hard deadline: the day the campaign starts. The
proposal defines the sections, the form, the reviews and the schedule to meet
that date. If the campaign changes along the way, we explain the adjustment and
its cost before making it.

- Campaign goal, content and date defined in writing.
- Form fields and data destination agreed from the start.
- Reviews included in the project schedule.
- Publishing account and access registered to your company.
