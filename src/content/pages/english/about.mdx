## What we do

We design, build, redesign and maintain websites, Shopify stores, landing pages and email templates. Before work begins, we agree in writing on the scope, cost, schedule and the accounts that remain under the client's control.

## Verifiable experience

Fernando Peralta, founder and lead of DevStoreMX, has more than seven years of web development experience. The team has contributed to more than 20 projects and to the operation of over 30 corporate and financial-sector websites. We also work as Shopify Partners on ecommerce projects.

## How we work

We divide every project into reviewable stages. We share progress, document scope changes and publish only after approval. The domain, hosting and main accounts remain in the client's name.

## Where we work

We are a remote agency. We serve organizations in English and Spanish across Mexico, Latin America, the United States, Canada and Europe.
