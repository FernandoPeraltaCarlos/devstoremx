faqItems:
  - question: "How do you calculate a project's cost?"
    answer: "Cost depends on scope, the number of pages or templates, functionality, integrations, available content and the target date. Before work begins, we provide a proposal that states what is included and excluded."
  - question: "How long does a website or Shopify store take?"
    answer: "The schedule is defined after reviewing the scope and content availability. Dates are agreed in writing and include review stages before publication."
  - question: "Will I own the domain, hosting and accounts?"
    answer: "Yes. The domain, hosting, Shopify and primary accounts remain under the client's control. We only request the permissions needed to complete the work."
  - question: "Can I review progress before publication?"
    answer: "Yes. We divide the project into reviewable stages, address feedback and publish only after approval."
  - question: "What do I need to provide before work begins?"
    answer: "We normally need goals, content, brand assets, existing access and one person responsible for approvals. The exact list is confirmed in each proposal."
  - question: "Do you work with existing websites?"
    answer: "Yes. We can review, redesign or maintain an existing website. If a focused correction solves the problem, we explain that before proposing a larger project."
  - question: "Do you provide support after launch?"
    answer: "We can agree on maintenance, updates and periodic checks as a separate service. Its scope and frequency are documented in writing."
  - question: "Do you work outside Mexico?"
    answer: "Yes. We are a remote agency serving clients in English and Spanish across Latin America, the United States, Canada and Europe."
